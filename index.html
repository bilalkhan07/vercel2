Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers regarding delivery times, file formats, copyright, and our human-design guarantee.

Are your designs genuinely 100% human-crafted without AI generators?

Yes, 100%. We enforce a strict, zero-tolerance policy against AI image tools like Midjourney or DALL-E. All graphics are handcrafted by vetted human designers in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Figma with structured vector paths, clean layer hierarchies, and unmerged typography.

How fast is the turnaround time and how do I receive my files?

Standard social media posts, YouTube thumbnails, vector tracings, and business cards are delivered in 30 to 60 minutes. As soon as you submit your brief, our system routes it to an active designer who connects with you immediately on WhatsApp to share initial drafts and final export files.

What file formats and source files do I get?

You receive full high-resolution exports (PNG, JPEG, print-ready 300 DPI CMYK PDF) along with the complete layered source files (Adobe Illustrator .AI, Photoshop .PSD, SVG, EPS, or Figma project link). You have 100% full commercial copyright ownership.

How do revisions work if I need changes?

Revisions are fast and unlimited! Since you are chatting directly with your designer on WhatsApp, you can share voice notes, reference images, or text updates. Your designer will apply tweaks in real-time until you are completely satisfied with the final result.

Can I request designs for local Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, etc.)?

Yes! Our Indian graphic designers are fluent in Devanagari and regional typography. We regularly design festive posters, retail banners, and social posts in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, and Punjabi.

Which cities in India do you cover?

We serve clients across 16+ key Indian cities including Indore, Bhopal, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Surat, Lucknow, Chandigarh, and Kochi, as well as businesses worldwide.
